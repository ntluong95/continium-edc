## Welcome to the Continium Documentation!

Continium is a versatile open-source platform for collecting and analyzing feedback from customers, users, and employees through targeted surveys. Whether you need simple forms or complex experience management solutions, Continium scales with your needs.

This guide covers everything you need to set up, use, and develop with Continium. You'll find step-by-step instructions, feature explanations, and best practices. It also includes advanced docs on extending Continium using its self-hosted option, APIs, and SDKs.
